# Tre Tarpley — Brand & Edit Style

Tre is the on-camera face of Agent Lead Lab's video ads to life insurance agents. Reference: "Tre 3.mp4", a 75-second vertical talking-head ad. When the team asks for a Tre script, an edit brief, captions, a thumbnail or a "Tre-style" video, follow this kit. Match it unless the team asks for something different.

## Who he's talking to
- Producing life insurance agents and agency owners, often in a named IMO ("If you're a Family First Life agent…"), who already write business and want better leads.
- He talks to them peer to peer: a confident operator, not a hype man. He's direct and calm, with a slight smile, and says "we".

## Voice & script formula (≈60–75 sec, ~190 words)
1. **Hook (0–3s):** call out who it's for plus a money qualifier. "If you're a [IMO] agent doing over $[X] per month…"
2. **Offer with risk reversal (3–11s):** "let me give you an offer you really can't lose on." Then stack it: no contract, no retainer, no setup fees, a 3X return on our leads in your first 30 days or you don't pay.
3. **Why we're confident / authority (11–24s):** time in business plus results at scale ("over four years… helped 700 agents hit $40,000 per month"), then "we own all the data, backend, references and trainings… depending on where you're at in your journey."
4. **Named case study (25–33s):** "Now, take Drake." Give spend vs. result: spent ~$1,600–$2,000 and wrote $12,000+ in annual premium with a variety of leads.
5. **Differentiator (33–55s):** a relationship, not just leads. Leads are text verified and fully compliant, and the team knows the agent and their agency, so agents know what's on the ad and what's in the process. "Bridge the gap most lead vendors don't."
6. **Objection handle (55–68s):** "You keep hearing me name drop these agents as if we're making them up, but…" Then the proof volume: 1,000+ videos on the website, hundreds of YouTube videos.
7. **CTA (69–75s):** "If you're interested in seeing how this works and want to work with the best lead company in the game, click Learn More below."
- Sentences are short and spoken. He uses "Now," and "And the reason why…" as transitions. There's no jargon he wouldn't say out loud.
- **Claims:** only use numbers the team has approved: the 3X / 30-day guarantee, 700 agents, $40K/month, 4 years, 1,000+ videos, and real named wins with their real figures. Never invent results, agent names or guarantees. If a new claim is needed, flag it for approval.

## Look (shoot)
- 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 30fps. One continuous talking-head take: medium close-up framed chest-up and centered, eye-level, looking straight into the lens.
- Bright, soft, natural daylight. Clean light-grey wall, with a gold-framed abstract marble/gold artwork on the left as the background accent.
- Wardrobe: black oversized tee with the white serif **UNBREAKABLE** wordmark and an underline across the chest. Tattooed forearms, hands clasped at the waist between gestures. A small clip-on mic is visible at the collar.

## Edit style (what editors should match)
- **Captions:** word-by-word, 1–3 words on screen at a time, synced to speech with no lag. ALL CAPS, white, in a very bold, wide/extended sans (Druk Wide / Clash Display Bold / Montserrat Black extended feel). No outline or box, just a subtle shadow. Centered horizontally, at about 45% of frame height: on the chest just above the UNBREAKABLE logo, never covering his face.
- **Hook treatment (first ~3s only):** a bigger, heavier, slightly italic wide font in mixed case. Key words are in **neon green #12FC88** (Agent Lead Lab green), e.g. "Family First Life Agent", "$30,000 per month". Small white lead-in words above them ("If you're a", "doing over"). After the hook, every caption goes to white caps.
- **Numbers stay as numerals** in captions ($30,000 · 3X · 30 DAYS · 700 AGENTS · $40,000 · $1,600 · $12,000 · 1,000 VIDEOS).
- **Proof overlays:** when he cites results, cut to real screenshots of the team's Discord **"NEW WIN"** posts (agent, carrier, lead type, AP, agent MTD, a LEADERBOARD link). Two styles:
  - a stack of win cards on a phone mock, centered, with the background blurred and tinted (warm orange or darkened), with the caption below it (e.g. "WE'VE HELPED · 700 AGENTS · $40,000 · PER MONTH");
  - a single win card for a named case study, sliding/popping in over the lower third while he keeps talking, held for the whole story beat (Drake: AP $816, Text Verified WIDOW, Americo).
- **Pacing:** fast but not chaotic. Use jump cuts to remove every pause and breath, with subtle punch-in/out zooms (~5–10%) every few lines to reset attention. There are no B-roll stock clips, transitions or emojis. Proof comes from real screenshots only.
- **CTA end card:** in the last ~5s, a white rounded **"Learn More"** pill button pops in under the caption, and an animated cursor clicks it on "click Learn More".
- **Audio:** his voice is clean and up front. Use no music, or a very low minimal bed. A soft pop/whoosh on overlays is fine; there are no meme sounds.
- **Color:** natural and bright. Keep skin tones true, with whites slightly cool and clean. Don't use heavy LUTs.

## Thumbnails / statics in his style
Tre centered, chest-up, in the black UNBREAKABLE tee against the light wall with the gold art. Add one short wide-font line in white caps with the money/number word in #12FC88 (e.g. "3X IN 30 DAYS"). Optionally add one Discord win card as proof. Use generic models in AI images; never generate Tre's face. Composite real photos of Tre in Canva.

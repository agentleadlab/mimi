# Copied Ads — Copyright Reports to Meta

Use this when a teammate shares a Facebook/Instagram page, post or ad that copies our ads (Tre's videos, our creative, our scripts, our images). The goal: one accurate, specific report per copy, filed through Meta's copyright form, plus a log entry so we can follow up. Never mass-report, never report pages that aren't copying us, and never exaggerate. Inaccurate reports get ignored and can hurt our accounts.

## What to collect (ask only for what's missing)
1. **The copy:** the exact URL of each infringing post, video, reel or ad (Ad Library links like facebook.com/ads/library/?id=… are ideal), plus the page name and page URL. Screenshots help.
2. **Our original:** the URL of our original post, ad or video, or the file or Loom, and roughly when we first published it.
3. **What was copied:** the whole video, a re-upload with their logo, our script read word for word, our thumbnail or images, our text.
If they give screenshots, compare the copy with our original and describe the specific matches (same footage, same script lines, same edit, logo swapped). Don't claim matches you can't see.

## Which route
- **Copyright** (this playbook): our video, image, creative or written ad copy reused. Form: https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/copyright (choose "Copyright", then the content type).
- If the page is also **pretending to be Agent Lead Lab or Tre**, note that it also needs an impersonation report. That's a separate step, so flag it and don't mix it into the copyright report.
- If the only overlap is a similar idea or angle (no copied footage, images or wording), say plainly that copyright usually won't cover it, and don't draft a report.

## The draft (post in Discord, field by field, ready to paste)
Title: "Copyright report: <page name>"
- **Content type:** video / photo / ad / text
- **Where the infringing content is:** each URL on its own line
- **Our original work:** URL(s), plus one line describing it (e.g. "60-sec talking-head video of Tre Tarpley, first published on the Agent Lead Lab page on Sept 12, 2026")
- **Description for Meta (2–4 sentences, factual):** what they copied, how it matches ours, and that it's used without permission to advertise. No insults, guesses or legal threats.
- **Filled in by the submitter (not Mimi):** rights owner's name, address, email, phone, relationship to the rights owner, the good-faith and accuracy statements, and the electronic signature. Tre (or whoever is authorized for Agent Lead Lab) must fill these in personally. Never invent them.

Then call log_copy_report to record it, and tell them the report ID (e.g. "CR-7") so they can update it: "Mimi, CR-7 submitted", "CR-7 removed".

## Following up
- Meta replies by email with a report number. Ask the teammate to add it: "Mimi, CR-7 submitted, Meta #123…".
- If Meta rejects a report, re-check the evidence once. If it's solid, suggest an appeal with clearer side-by-side proof. Don't refile the same report repeatedly.
- The same page copying again later gets a new report for the new content, linked in the notes.
